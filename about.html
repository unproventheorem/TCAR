About Us

Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue started from an idea that all animals should be provided with the same basic care and necessities as people. We grew from a few staff in a single location to now having over 45 staff members across the county. We take pride in what we do, and will continue to expand to provide our services across the state.

Core Values

  • Compassion: Showing love to every animal.
  • Dedication: Never giving up on finding the perfect home.
  • Community: Working together with volunteers, fosters, and donors.
  • Hope: Believing every animal deserves a second chance.

Thank you to our partners

A huge thank you to everyone that supports us, from our foster families, to our volunteers and pet food donors. Everyone that helps to support animal rescue has an impact. Thank you!
